Racing FAQ

Everything before you race

Key answers from EasyKart Thailand’s FAQ — prices, bookings, kids, safety and formats. Full FAQ lives on easykart.net.

Do I need to book in advance?

At EasyKart Pattaya (Bali Hai Pier and Thepprasit) you can walk in during opening hours. Online booking gives faster check-in. Races start every 10 minutes with open-time tickets.

How long is each race?

Each race lasts 8 minutes — about 10 to 15 laps depending on your speed. Multi-race packages are available for a longer session.

What are the standard prices?

Kid Kart from 499 THB (1 race). Regular and 2-Seater from 699 THB (1 race). Fast Kart package from 1,499 THB (2 races). Confirm current packages on the booking page.

What should I wear?

Sport / closed-toe shoes are mandatory. Shoe rental is 50 THB per pair (all sizes, includes new socks). Helmets included; racing suits free if you want the full look.

Opening hours?

Both Pattaya tracks: daily 10:00 – 01:00 (after midnight). Outdoor locations with panoramic viewing areas.

Where else is EasyKart?

EasyKart operates since 2004 across Thailand: Bangkok (RCA Plaza), Pattaya Bali Hai Pier, Pattaya Thepprasit, and Koh Samui (Chaweng Lake).
